Let’s break it down cleanly.

Short answer first: **service-based companies hire far more project managers than product-based companies**.
Product companies do hire PM roles, but the volume and frequency are much lower.

Now the why, and then the actual company names.

---

## Why service companies dominate PM hiring

Here’s the thing.
Service companies run **multiple client projects at the same time**. Every project needs planning, delivery, coordination, reporting, and escalation handling. That creates constant demand for:

* Project Manager
* Associate / Deputy Project Manager
* Project Lead
* IT Manager
* Delivery Manager
* Program Manager

Product companies focus on **one or a few products**. Fewer parallel projects. Fewer PM roles. More emphasis on Product Managers rather than Project Managers.

What this really means is:
If your goal is **faster entry, more openings, and steady growth**, service-based firms are the safer bet.

## IT Manager / IT Head roles — where they come from

Most IT Managers and IT Heads are **grown inside service companies**.

Typical path:
Project Lead → Project Manager → Delivery Manager → IT Manager → IT Head

Service firms provide:

* Team management experience
* Client ownership
* Budget responsibility

That’s why senior IT leadership mostly comes from services, not products.
